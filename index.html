LANDLOCKED

Privacy policy

Effective 2nd of August 2026 · Developer: btuzel

Landlocked is an offline geography game. Your play stays on your device.

Data collection and sharing

Landlocked does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or network permission.

Data stored on your device

The app stores daily puzzle progress, routes, lives, results, and elapsed time locally using Android DataStore. This information never reaches the developer or a third party. You can delete it by clearing Landlocked's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Android backup is disabled for Landlocked.

Third-party services and data sources

Landlocked does not connect to third-party services. Country names, border relationships, and silhouettes are bundled with the app. Dataset licensing is separate from user data processing and is documented in the app's open-source attribution file.

Children

Landlocked is a general-audience geography game and is not designed specifically for children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

Changes and contact

If Landlocked's data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated before the change is released. Questions can be raised through the Landlocked issue tracker.
